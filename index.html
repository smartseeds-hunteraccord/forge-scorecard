The five criteria

Every Accord project is rated Bronze, Silver or Gold on five criteria. Tap a criterion to see what Forge committed to and what Gold would take.

Bronze is passed on the way to Silver. Dashed = not yet reached.

Why Forge rates Silver overall

Forge meets the Silver standard on every criterion: jobs and skills, community investment, community voice, waste and energy. None of its commitments reach Gold yet, so its overall rating is Silver.
